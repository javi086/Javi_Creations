Aurora Ice Wine

375ml / 10% alc. Vol.

$100.00 CAD

(Before taxes)

Aurora Ice Wine, produced by Lutton Farm Partnership t/a Aurora English Blueberry Wine, is a unique and exquisite dessert wine crafted from 100% English blueberries. This ice wine showcases the natural sweetness and rich, concentrated flavors that develop when the blueberries are harvested at their peak and then frozen before fermentation.

The wine boasts a vibrant color and offers a palate that is intensely fruity with balancing acidity, making it an excellent companion for desserts or as a standalone treat to savor. The meticulous production process and artisanal care taken in creating this wine reflect the dedication of the Lutton Farm Partnership to high-quality, locally sourced ingredients.
